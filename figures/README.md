# Figures

Plots produced by `notebooks/T3_T4.ipynb` (PDF, used in the report).

- `degree_ccdf.pdf`: in- and out-degree distributions of both graphs (CCDF)
- `pagerank_ccdf.pdf`: distribution of N · PageRank in both graphs (CCDF)
- `target_hist.pdf`: histogram of the target y = log(N · PageRank)
- `target_vs_indegree.pdf`: target against log(1 + in-degree), with the baseline line
- `perturbation_curve.pdf`: Spearman and top-10% of the two references on the perturbed graphs
- `top100_train.gexf`: the 100 articles with the highest PageRank and their links, for Gephi
